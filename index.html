About

I'm a PhD student and research assistant in computational social science, studying how language moves through social media — how discourse shifts, how stance gets signals, how frames get built and contested online. My work sits at the intersection of NLP and political communication.

Outside the lab, I'm usually at a piano. I'm deep in jazz improvisation right now, which is teaching me more about structure and surprise than any paper I've read. Sundays I play in my church worship band. I'm also a blue belt in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, which sounds unrelated to research until you realize how much both are about reading what's actually happening versus what you expected to happen.

I grew up in Dallas and still ride my road bike out to White Rock Lake when the weather cooperates — good for clearing headspace and building up a lactic acid problem I'll regret on the mat the next morning.
